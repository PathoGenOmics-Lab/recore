    Summary

    8 binary matrix cells were corrected under the Balanced policy from 39 searchable gaps.     Output directory: tutorial_out

    Using these results

    False gene absences in the gene presence/absence matrix (24 clusters, 12 genomes) were corrected with Recore v0.2.0. Each absent cell of a cluster present in at least 10% of genomes was searched by aligning the cluster's representative sequence and every allele to that genome's assembly with minimap2 (preset asm5); a hit counted when it reached at least 90% identity over at least 80% of the query. Hits were classified against the genome annotation as missing annotations, split or fragmented genes, start variants or pseudogenes, and kept only when the flanking gene context (synteny) agreed with the cluster's. Under the balanced matrix policy, 8 cells were set to present; 3 further cells with sequence evidence were kept out of the binary matrix, and the synteny and paralog guards rejected 0 candidates. The evidence behind every corrected and kept-out cell is listed in orthology_evidence.tsv.

    • 3 cells have sequence evidence but are not in the corrected matrix (3 by the Balanced policy, 0 by the quality floors). They are drawn as kept out and carry publication_status held_out_policy or held_out_quality.
    • 11 gaps were never searched (11 in clusters below the 10% prevalence floor). They are absent because the input matrix says so, not because Recore looked: the states file writes them as NOT_SEARCHED and the matrix draws them hatched.

    Changed cells

    The list of changed cells needs the interactive workspace; orthology_evidence.tsv has every row.
